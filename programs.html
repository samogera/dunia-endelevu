For schools · Now in pilot

Early warning for schools.

Heat, dusty or smoky air and heavy rain put children at risk at school. Our early warning service tells schools in good time, so they can keep pupils safe and learning.

  1. 1

    We watch

    We follow open air-quality readings and official weather forecasts for each school’s area.

  2. 2

    We alert

    When the air turns unhealthy, or heat or heavy rain is on the way, the head teacher gets a clear alert with a suggested step, in line with the school’s own health rules.

  3. 3

    The school acts

    The school confirms the alert and notes what it did, such as moving PE indoors or keeping pupils in at break.

  4. 4

    Everyone is ready

    Parents can be told ahead of time, and partners are alerted when a school needs extra help.

Example alert

Air quality: unhealthy for sensitive groups. Suggested step: move today’s PE lesson indoors and check on pupils with asthma.

We are piloting the service now. We record only what each school did, never pupils’ names.

Anything else? Email hello@duniaendelevu.org.

Who can join?

Any child under 18, through a partner school or one of our tree-planting events. Schools can apply to become partners.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Donations, sponsorships and anthology sales pay for books, recordings and travel to events.

What do you need from parents?

The child’s name, age and school, and the parent or guardian’s name, relationship, phone number and email. Before the first session, the parent or guardian signs a consent form.

When and where are sessions?

Weekly, for one school term, at partner schools and public venues, always with vetted adults.
