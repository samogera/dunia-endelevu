Your right to a healthy environment.

Clean air, clean water and a safe place to live are not just nice to have. In Kenya, they are your right.

What are rights?

Rights are things every person should have and be protected to have, like education, health and safety. Children have rights too.

Kenya’s Constitution

Article 42 of Kenya’s Constitution (2010) says every person has the right to a clean and healthy environment. That includes you.

Around the world

In 2022, the United Nations agreed that a clean, healthy and sustainable environment is a human right. In 2023, a United Nations committee on children’s rights said governments must protect children from harm to the environment, including climate change.

Children also have the right to be heard on things that affect them.

Rights come with responsibilities

Kenya’s Constitution also says every person has a duty to help protect and look after the environment.

If the environment near you is harmed, tell a trusted adult. Adults can report it to the county government or to NEMA, Kenya’s environment authority.

Words to know

Right
Something every person should have and be protected to have.
Responsibility
Something you should do to help others and the planet.
Constitution
The most important set of rules for a country.
NEMA
The National Environment Management Authority, which looks after Kenya’s environment.
