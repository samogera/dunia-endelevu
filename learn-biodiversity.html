The web of life.

Biodiversity means all the different living things on Earth. Here is why every one of them matters.

What is biodiversity?

“Bio” means life and “diversity” means many different kinds. Biodiversity is all the plants, animals, insects, fungi and tiny microbes on Earth, and the places where they live.

Everything is connected

Living things depend on each other, like threads in a web. Bees carry pollen from flower to flower, so plants can make fruits and seeds, like mangoes and avocados.

Birds spread those seeds, and new trees grow. Pull one thread, and others are affected.

Kenya is full of life

Kenya has savannahs with giraffes and lions, Kakamega Forest (a tropical rainforest in western Kenya), Lake Nakuru with its flamingos, and coral reefs at Watamu on the coast.

What harms it, and how we help

Cutting down forests, plastic and rubbish, pollution and climate change all harm wildlife.

We help by planting indigenous trees, growing flowers for bees and keeping rubbish out of nature. Kenya plans to grow 15 billion trees by 2032.

Words to know

Biodiversity
All the different kinds of life on Earth.
Habitat
The home where a plant or animal lives.
Pollinator
An animal, like a bee or butterfly, that carries pollen between flowers.
Indigenous tree
A tree that grows naturally in Kenya.
Ecosystem
Living things and their surroundings, working together.
