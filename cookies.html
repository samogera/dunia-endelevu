Cookie Policy

The short version: we don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and the website works without any cookies at all.

Last updated 25 October 2026
At a glance No tracking cookies No analytics No adverts, ever Podcast player sets its own

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They can remember settings, keep you signed in, or track what you do across websites.

The cookies on this website

TypeDo we use it?Details
EssentialNoThe website does not need cookies to work.
AnalyticsNoWe don’t measure visitors with analytics tools.
AdvertisingNeverWe don’t show adverts or allow advertising trackers.
Third-party servicesOnly when you use themIf you play our podcast in the Spotify player, or send a form, those services may set their own cookies. Their own cookie policies apply.

Our fonts are stored on our own website, so loading a page does not share your details with a font provider.

Children

We never use cookies or any other technology to profile children or show them adverts.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. In Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, look under Settings, then Privacy. Blocking cookies won’t stop this website working, though the embedded podcast player may not play.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or any other cookies, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Questions

Email hello@duniaendelevu.org. For how we handle personal information, see our Privacy Policy.
