Children aren’t just in the room. They’re on the stage.

Dunia Endelevu is a youth-led climate incubator in Kenya. We help children publish their own books, speak on our global podcast and take the stage at major environmental events.

We don’t just teach children about climate change. We give them the platform to lead the conversation.

When decisions about the planet’s future are made, children should not just be in the room, but on the stage. We give young advocates the coaching, the platform and the funding to get there, at no cost to their families.

How the journey works

  1. 01JoinThrough a partner school or a tree-planting event, with a parent’s or guardian’s consent.
  2. 02Choose a trackStoryteller, Voice or Diplomat, chosen together with the child.
  3. 03TrainWeekly coaching in writing, speaking, interviewing and climate facts.
  4. 04LeadA published book, a broadcast episode or a speech on a real stage.

Our mission

To give children the skills, the platform and the funding to lead the climate conversation.

Our vision

A world where children’s voices shape climate decisions, from Nairobi classrooms to global summits.

The Dunia Endelevu Climate Anthology

Ten young authors. One book. Every year. Short climate stories by children in our Storyteller Track, professionally edited and illustrated. Every copy sold to a school, library or donor funds next year’s authors.
