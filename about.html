A sustainable world, led by the children who will live in it.

Dunia Endelevu is a youth-led social enterprise and incubator based in Kenya. We turn children’s climate ideas into books, broadcasts and speeches the world can hear.

Our name is Kiswahili, so the message starts close to home.

Endelevusustainable, lasting

Mission

Give children the skills, platform and funding to lead the climate conversation, not just learn about it.

Vision

When decisions about the planet’s future are made, children are not just in the room, but on the stage.

Model

A social enterprise. Partnerships, sponsorships and book sales pay for the programme, so it stays free for every child.

Why we exist

Climate change already shapes children’s schooling, health and safety, yet children are rarely heard when climate decisions are made. Most never get the chance to write a book, speak on air or stand at a podium.

Dunia Endelevu changes that with a clear path, real coaching and the funding to make it happen, for children across Kenya.

Our safeguarding promise

Every child in our programme is under 18. Keeping them safe comes before everything else we do.

To raise a concern, email hello@duniaendelevu.org with “Safeguarding” in the subject line. Our safeguarding lead reads every message.

  1. 01Every child joins with written consent from a parent or legal guardian, and that consent can be withdrawn at any time.
  2. 02Sessions take place in schools or public venues, supervised by vetted adults. No adult is ever alone with a child.
  3. 03We publish children’s work under their first name and age only, and never share a child’s contact details.
  4. 04Photos, recordings and stories are published only with separate written permission for each use.
  5. 05Every child travelling to an event is accompanied by a parent, guardian or approved chaperone.
  6. 06Children’s personal data is handled in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. Read our Privacy Policy.
