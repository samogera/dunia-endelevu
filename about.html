About us

A sustainable world, led by the children who will live in it.

Dunia Endelevu is a youth-led climate incubator in Kenya. We help children write, speak and lead on climate, for free.

Who we are

Our name means “a sustainable world”.

Dunia is Kiswahili for “the world”. Endelevu means “sustainable, lasting”.

Climate change will shape the lives of today’s children more than anyone’s, yet they are rarely asked what they think. We change that. Children under 18 join one of three free tracks: they publish their own climate book, co-host our podcast Climate Gumzo, or speak at real events. Trained adults coach them, keep them safe and raise the money, so families never pay.

What we believe

Three simple ideas.

01

Children are experts on their own future.

They see the problem clearly. They deserve to be asked.

02

Every young voice deserves a real audience.

Not a classroom exercise. A real book, a real broadcast, a real stage.

03

No family should pay to be heard.

Donors and sponsors cover every cost, so talent is all a child needs.

Founder

Alana Chelangat

Climate justice advocate, author and podcaster from Kenya.

Safety

Safe, always.

Every child in our programmes is under 18. Their safety comes first, every time.

To raise a concern, email hello@duniaendelevu.org with “Safeguarding” in the subject line. Our safeguarding lead reads every message.

Who can join the programmes?

Children under 18, with written consent from a parent or legal guardian. Adults can’t join the programmes, but they can volunteer or donate.

How do you keep children safe?

Every adult is vetted before working with children. There are always at least two adults at a session, so a child is never alone with one adult. A parent, guardian or approved chaperone goes with every child to events.

Will my child’s name or photo be published?

Only with your written permission, given separately for each use. We credit children by first name and age only, and never publish contact details.

Who checks the volunteers?

We do. Volunteers must be 18 or over, are vetted before they start and follow our safeguarding policy. They never work one-to-one with a child.

Can I withdraw my child?

Yes, at any time. Email us and we stop. We also remove your child’s work from our website and from future editions.

How is my child’s information protected?

We follow Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, and only the people who need it can see it. Read our Privacy Policy.
