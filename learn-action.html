What can we do?

Climate action means slowing climate change, and getting ready for the changes already here.

Two kinds of action

Some actions cut the pollution that warms the planet: saving energy, using clean energy and planting trees.

Others help us get ready for changes: saving rainwater for dry times, planting shade trees and warning people early about very hot days or heavy rain.

At home

Switch off lights and devices nobody is using. Turn off the tap while you brush your teeth. Reuse and repair things instead of throwing them away.

At school

Grow a tree nursery. Alana, our founder, co-founded a school tree nursery with more than 20,000 trees. Compost food scraps, collect rainwater, and start an environment club or a daily weather board.

Use your voice

Write a story, speak at assembly or ask a question on a podcast. Dunia Endelevu helps children do all three, for free.

The world is acting too

In 2015, almost every country agreed to work together to slow global warming. This is called the Paris Agreement. Kenya banned plastic carrier bags in 2017.

Words to know

Mitigation
Cutting the pollution that causes climate change.
Adaptation
Getting ready for the changes climate change brings.
Renewable energy
Energy that does not run out, like sun, wind, water and geothermal.
Compost
Food scraps that rot down into rich soil.
