We don’t just teach children about climate change. We give them the platform to lead the conversation.

When decisions about the planet’s future are made, children should not just be in the room, but on the stage. We give young advocates the coaching, the platform and the funding to get there, at no cost to their families.

How the journey works

  1. 01JoinThrough a partner school or a tree-planting event, with a parent’s or guardian’s consent.
  2. 02Choose a trackStoryteller, Voice or Diplomat, chosen together with the child.
  3. 03TrainWeekly coaching in writing, speaking, interviewing and climate facts.
  4. 04LeadA published book, a broadcast episode or a speech on a real stage.
