Safe, always.

Every child is under 18. Their safety comes first.

To raise a concern, email {{email}} with “Safeguarding” in the subject line. Our safeguarding lead reads every message.

  • Written consent from a parent or guardian, withdrawable any time.
  • Vetted adults at every session. Never one-to-one.
  • First name and age only. Never contact details.
  • Written permission for every photo and recording.
  • A chaperone with every child at events.
  • Children’s data is handled under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019. Privacy Policy
